About

We started in livestock management, which is probably why we still think in rotational cycles and view fence lines as load-bearing. Somewhere around 2016, We expanded into agricultural production to diversify our offering and better serve our community.

These days, we focus on the kind of farm staples people rely on without thinking twice — feed grains, pasture management, and everyday livestock care. Unglamorous, and we like it that way.
